- [GSE104101](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE104101)

Please cite DeBarry JD et al. MaHPIC malaria systems biology data from
*Plasmodium cynomolgi* sporozoite longitudinal infections in macaques.
*Scientific Data* 9, 722 (2022).
[DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01755-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01755-y).

The repository's `LICENSE` covers the original software. The workbooks remain
third-party GEO submissions. [NCBI's data policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/)
permits use and distribution of deposited molecular data while recognizing
that submitters may retain intellectual-property rights.

`manifest.json` records the SHA-256 hashes of the included workbooks.
